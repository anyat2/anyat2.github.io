# Findings
We found that nuisance complaints were not evenly distributed across time or location. When grouping complaints by season, there is a noticeable increase in reports during the spring months compared to other parts of the year. This suggests that seasonal factors may influence when residents are more likely to report issues or when nuisance activity is more prevalent.

In addition to seasonal trends, the data shows variation across housing blocks. Some blocks consistently experience a higher number of complaints than others, indicating that certain locations may face more persistent issues. These differences highlight that nuisance complaints are not randomly distributed but instead tend to cluster in specific areas and times.

Overall, the patterns suggest that both environmental and location-based factors may play a role in shaping when and where nuisance complaints occur.

Overview
This project analyzes a dataset of nuisance complaints reported across different housing blocks. Each row in the dataset represents a single complaint submitted by a resident, including information such as the type of issue reported, the housing block where it occurred, the date it was reported, and the final disposition of the case. Before analysis, the data was cleaned to remove missing or inconsistent values so that patterns could be more accurately identified.

The dataset consists of individual nuisance complaint records, where each entry represents a single reported incident. Key variables include the housing block where the complaint occurred, the type of complaint submitted, the date it was reported, and the final disposition describing how the complaint was handled. Together, these showings allow us to understand not just how many complaints were made, but also where they happened,the season,  what issues were most common, and how they were resolved. These variables were standardized so they could be compared consistently across the dataset, making it possible to identify meaningful patterns in both location and timing.
