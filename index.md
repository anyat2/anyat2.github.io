This site contains my data visualization project 

Nuisance Complaints in the Urbana Champaign area

This dataset examines nuisance complaints reported across different housing blocks and complaint categories. The visualizations help show where complaints are most concentrated and which issues are reported most frequently. By organizing the data by housing block and type of complaint, we can better understand patterns in neighborhood concerns and identify areas that may require additional city attention or maintenance. The dataset includes information such as complaint type, housing block location, and the number of complaints submitted over time.
The first visualization displays the number of complaints reported by housing block. The chart shows that certain housing blocks experience significantly more complaints than others. Housing blocks such as 700, 800, and 1200 reported the highest number of complaints, while many other blocks had relatively low complaint counts. This uneven distribution suggests that nuisance issues are concentrated in specific areas rather than being equally spread throughout the city. These differences may reflect variations in population density, maintenance conditions, environmental factors, or neighborhood activity levels. The interactive visualization also allows users to hover over the chart to explore more detailed information about complaint counts for each block.
The second visualization focuses on the types of complaints submitted. The most common complaint category by far was “Weeds/Grass,” followed by “Municipal Waste.” Other complaint categories such as zoning issues, snow removal, tenant complaints, and neighborhood concerns appeared much less frequently. This indicates that environmental and property maintenance issues are the dominant nuisance concerns within the dataset. The findings suggest that seasonal outdoor conditions and waste management may play a major role in resident complaints. Overall, the dataset highlights how complaint data can be used to identify recurring community issues and help local agencies prioritize maintenance and public service efforts.
